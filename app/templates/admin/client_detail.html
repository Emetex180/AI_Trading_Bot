Removing an account is reversible. Restoring it brings the account back and leaves its payment history untouched. The holder will need to sign in again, because their previous session was invalidated when the account was removed — which is what made the removal take effect immediately rather than at the next session expiry.

{% elif c.id == current_user.id %} {# The route refuses this as well — the message is here so the operator understands why there is no button, rather than looking for one. #}

You cannot remove the account you are signed in with. If it should be removed, sign in as another administrator and remove it from there — otherwise the deployment would have no way back into its own administration.

{% else %}

The account stops working immediately: it cannot sign in, it disappears from the client list, and every gated page refuses it. Nothing it recorded is destroyed — its subscriptions and payments are kept, because those are the records needed if a charge is ever disputed.
