{% extends "admin/base.html" %} {% block title %}Subscriptions{% endblock %} {# Read-only by design. A subscription is granted in exactly two ways — a payment settling, or an admin editing the client's profile — and both already have their own route. A third one that flipped a status directly would be a way to hand out access with no payment behind it and no audit line. What this page lists is the *first* of those two: rows in ``subscriptions``, every one of which is written by ``Repository.activate_subscription`` and so every one of which has a payment behind it. The second way is an edit to ``client_profiles.subscription_plan``, which is not a row here at all — it is shown as the account's effective plan on its detail page. The page says so rather than implying it covers both, because an operator who read it as a full account of who has access would miss every hand-granted account. #} {% block content %}
