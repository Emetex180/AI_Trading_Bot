Features

What the platform does

Everything below reads from the same engine that produces the signals. The platform is a view onto the strategy's state, not a second opinion about it.

Live market

Every instrument, and where it has got to

{{ figures.monitored_assets }} instruments, each showing the step it has reached in the model: watching, liquidity purged, CISD confirmed, FVG formed, waiting on a retracement, retracement confirmed, or a setup published. An instrument with no setup yet is shown as such — the state is the information.

Setups

Signals that reached the end of the sequence

A setup appears only once confirmation completes and the risk gate approves it. Each one carries direction, entry, stop, target and the ratio between them, so you can judge it rather than take it on faith.

Detail

The reasoning, not just the outcome

Every setup opens into the checklist that produced it — each condition marked met or unmet — alongside the liquidity level and the imbalance zone the decision was made against.

History

A record you can filter

Every setup the engine has produced, filterable by instrument, by outcome and by date range. Useful for reviewing how the model behaves in conditions you care about.

Analysis

A workspace for the wider picture

A consolidated view of engine output over time, with performance and backtest reporting. Available on the VIP plan.

Time

One clock across the whole platform

Every timestamp, session window and filter is New York local time, daylight-saving aware. The platform and your charts read the same clock without you converting anything.

Account

Your account, under your control

Update your name, email, phone, country and photo from your profile. A password change ends every other session on the account immediately.

Security

Built to be held to account

Passwords are stored only as hashes. Every page checks what your account is entitled to on the server, so hiding a button is never what keeps you out of something. Card details never reach 3rader — Flutterwave handles the payment.

What 3rader is not

Worth being direct about, because the difference matters more than the feature list.

It does not trade for you

No broker connection, no order placement, no money management. You decide what to do with a setup and you execute it yourself.

It does not give financial advice

Setups are the output of a mechanical model applied to market data. They are not personal recommendations and they take no account of your circumstances.

It does not promise a result

No win rate, no return figure and no guarantee appears anywhere on this site or inside the platform. Trading carries risk of loss.
