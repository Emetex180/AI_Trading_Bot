{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ # 1. The problem ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
The problem

The market moves whether you are watching or not.

Watching four sessions a day is a job in itself, and most of what happens on a chart is noise. The setups worth acting on have to meet every condition first — and none of them announce themselves in advance.

  • Setups arrive while you are away A window that qualifies at 03:00 does not wait until you open a chart.
  • Hours spent on charts that never qualify Most instruments produce nothing on most sessions. Sitting through them is the cost, and it is paid whether or not anything comes of it.
  • Not knowing whether the rules were actually met A direction on its own cannot be checked. Without the conditions behind it, you are taking someone's word for the part that matters.
  • Second-guessing the ones you do take Judgement made in the moment is hard to repeat, and hard to review afterwards.
  • Losing the record of what happened Memory keeps the winners and edits the rest, which is a poor basis for deciding what to change.
{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ # 2. The solution ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
The approach

3rader watches the market and brings qualifying setups to you.

The engine monitors every instrument continuously, works a fixed model on each one, and publishes a setup only when the last condition in the sequence is met and the risk gate approves it. You do not have to be watching for that to happen — and you do not have to take the result on trust, because the working is attached to it.

Continuous

Monitored, not sampled

Every instrument is watched through every session the model is built around, on one clock, so nothing depends on when you happened to look.

Fixed rules

The same method every time

A defined sequence of conditions, evaluated in the same order on every instrument. What changes is the market, not the method.

Qualified only

Most of the time, nothing

A setup is published only when every condition is met and the risk gate approves it. The platform says so rather than manufacturing activity to look busy.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ # 3. How it works ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
How it works

From monitoring to a decision you can review

Six steps, the same every session. The engine does the first four; the last two are yours.

Monitor

Every monitored instrument is watched continuously through {{ figures.session_names | join(', ') }}.

Analyse

Each instrument is measured against the model: liquidity, change in state of delivery, imbalance, structure.

Validate

A candidate must clear every condition in order and pass the risk gate. Most candidates do not, and are discarded.

Alert

A qualifying setup is published to your dashboard, with email and Telegram delivery on the plans that include them.

Decide

You review the setup, its levels and the conditions behind it, and make the call yourself.

Track

Every setup is recorded with its context, so you can review what the model did, and when, rather than relying on memory.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ # 5. Signal mode — the user makes the call ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
Signal mode

You stay in control of every decision.

3rader does not trade. It publishes what the model found — the direction, the levels, the ratio between them and the conditions behind it — and leaves the decision to you, where it belongs.

  • Nothing is executed on your behalf 3rader does not connect to your broker, does not place orders and does not manage money. There is no account of yours for it to act on.
  • Every setup shows its working Direction, entry, stop, target, the ratio between them, and each condition marked met or unmet with the levels it was judged against.
  • You can disagree with a step, not the whole thing Because the reasoning is visible, a setup you reject tells you something specific about why — which is what makes the record worth keeping.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ # 9. Questions ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
Questions

Before you sign up

Does 3rader trade for me?

No. 3rader analyses the market and publishes setups. It does not connect to your broker, does not place orders and does not manage money. Every decision to act is yours.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and you should be sceptical of anything that does. Trading involves risk of loss. 3rader applies a defined process consistently, which is a different thing from promising an outcome.

Which instruments are covered?

The platform monitors {{ figures.monitored_assets }} instruments across foreign exchange, metals, indices and crypto. The full list is visible inside the platform on the live market page.

Which sessions?

{{ figures.session_names | join(', ') }} — the periods the strategy is built around. All times are shown in New York local time.

How often does something qualify?

Rarely, and that is the point. A setup is published only when every condition is met and the risk gate approves it. Most instruments produce nothing on most sessions, and the platform shows an instrument waiting rather than inventing something for it to show.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Card payment is handled by Flutterwave; 3rader never sees or stores your card details. Subscriptions do not auto-renew, and you can cancel from your subscription page at any time — access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.

What do I need to get started?

An email address and a card, if you want a paid plan. Create an account, choose a plan, and the dashboard opens.
