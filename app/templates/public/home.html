What the engine actually looks for

Each instrument moves through the same state machine, once per session. A setup is only published after the last step, and only if it also passes the risk gate.

Liquidity purge

A one-hour candle closes beyond a liquidity level — a previous high or low where resting orders have built up.

Change in state of delivery

The five-minute series confirms the shift: the level that held before the purge gives way.

Fair value gap

The first qualifying one-minute imbalance is marked. This is the zone price is expected to return to.

Retracement

Price trades back into the gap. The setup is armed but not yet valid — a retracement alone is not a signal.

Confirmation

The entry condition completes inside the gap. Only now is the setup published, with its levels and its risk-to-reward.

Questions

Does 3rader trade for me?

No. 3rader analyses the market and publishes setups. It does not connect to your broker, does not place orders and does not manage money. Every decision to act is yours.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and you should be sceptical of anything that does. Trading involves risk of loss. 3rader applies a defined process consistently, which is a different thing from promising an outcome.
