{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ # 1. How it works — three steps, not six. The engine does all three. ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
How it works

Three steps. The same method every session.

No judgement in the moment, no alert for something that merely looks close. A setup reaches you only after the last condition in the sequence has been met and the risk gate has approved it.

Watch

Every monitored instrument is watched continuously through {{ figures.session_names | join(', ') }}, on one clock.

Validate

Each instrument is measured against a fixed sequence — liquidity, change in state of delivery, imbalance, retracement, confirmation — and must pass the risk gate. Most candidates do not, and are discarded.

Publish

A qualifying setup appears on your dashboard with its entry, stop, target, ratio and condition checklist, and is recorded in history so every decision can be reviewed afterwards.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ # 2. What a setup carries ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
The platform

What a confirmed setup carries

A direction with no working shown is not much use. Each setup arrives with its levels, the ratio between them, and the condition checklist the engine cleared to produce it — so you can disagree with one step rather than with the whole thing.

Around it: a live market view of where each instrument has reached in the model, a filterable record of everything produced, and an analysis workspace for reviewing the strategy against history.

3rader publishes analysis. It does not connect to your broker, place orders or manage money — the decision to act is always yours.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ # 6. Questions — the ones that stop a sign-up. ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
Questions

Before you sign up

Does 3rader trade for me?

No. 3rader analyses the market and publishes setups. It does not connect to your broker, does not place orders and does not manage money. Every decision to act is yours.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and you should be sceptical of anything that does. Trading involves risk of loss. 3rader applies a defined process consistently, which is a different thing from promising an outcome.

How often does something qualify?

Rarely, and that is the point. A setup is published only when every condition is met and the risk gate approves it. Most instruments produce nothing on most sessions, and the platform shows an instrument waiting rather than inventing something for it to show.

Which instruments and sessions are covered?

{{ figures.monitored_assets }} instruments across foreign exchange, metals, indices and crypto, watched through {{ figures.session_names | join(', ') }}. All times are shown in New York local time, and the full instrument list is visible on the live market page.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Card payment is handled by Flutterwave; 3rader never sees or stores your card details. Subscriptions do not auto-renew, and you can cancel from your subscription page at any time — access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.

Can 3rader run my own trading model?

Not yet. Custom model development is a planned future feature. Today the platform runs one fixed model, applied identically to every instrument.
