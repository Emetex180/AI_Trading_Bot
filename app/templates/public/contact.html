Reporting a vulnerability

Please write to {{ support_email }} with enough detail to reproduce the issue. We would rather hear about it than not, and we will not pursue anyone who reports in good faith.

We will never ask for your password. Nobody at 3rader can see it, and no legitimate message from us will request it, a card number or a one-time code. Treat any message that does as fraudulent.
