Before you pay

What happens when the 30 days end?

Your subscription lapses. You keep your account and your history, and the pages your plan covered become locked again until you start a new one. Nothing is charged automatically.

Can I change plans?

Yes — start the plan you want and it replaces the current one. Your remaining time on the old plan does not transfer, so it is usually worth changing at the end of a period rather than the start.

What if a payment fails?

Nothing is activated and nothing is charged for that attempt. You will get an email saying so, and you can try again from the pricing page.

Do you offer refunds?

Statutory rights apply where they exist. Beyond those, refunds are considered case by case — write to {{ support_email }} with your payment reference. The terms set out the position in full.
